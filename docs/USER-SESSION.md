# User session: one clinician, one synthetic discharge

Length: 30 minutes. Facilitator: the founder. One clinician, one synthetic patient, the step-5 screen in replay mode. Everything on screen is made up.

## A. Session script

### Before the clinician arrives

- Open the step-5 screen on the synthetic patient in bed 4. The agent has not started.
- Check whether voice works in this build. If it does not, the clinician types.
- Have the capture sheet open, one copy per task.
- Do not explain the screen, its parts or its labels.

### Plan

| Minute | Part |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 | Consent line (part C) and framing |
| 2 to 3 | Let them look at the screen in silence for one minute |
| 3 to 11 | Task 1 |
| 11 to 17 | Task 2 |
| 17 to 24 | Task 3 |
| 24 to 28 | Closing question |
| 28 to 30 | Thank-you, spare time |

### Framing (read aloud)

"This is an early prototype of an assistant that prepares discharge paperwork, and everything on the screen is made up. We are testing the prototype, not you, so please think aloud and say whatever comes to mind."

### Tasks (read each card aloud, then stay quiet)

Task 1: "Ask the agent to prepare the usual discharge paperwork for the patient in bed 4."

Task 2: "The doctor has not signed yet. Ask the agent where things stand."

Task 3: "The ward has just told you they now want the medicines listed as dispensed. Tell the agent."

### Facilitator rules

- Let them use their own words. Write down exactly what they say or type.
- If the prototype does not understand their wording, note the words, then offer the card wording once.
- Do not answer "is that right?" Reply "What do you think?"
- Neutral prompts only: "What are you thinking?", "What would you do now?", "What did you expect to happen?"
- If they are stuck for two minutes, help them, and log it on the sheet as an assist.
- Do not point at any part of the screen, and do not name any label on it.

### Closing question

"Of everything you saw today, what would you not trust, and why?"

Only if they need a nudge: "What would you need to see before you relied on it?"

## C. Consent and thank-you

Consent (read before anything else): "Everything in this session is synthetic, with no real patient data; we will not record audio or screen unless you agree now; my notes will not carry your name or your hospital; and you can stop at any time."

Recording agreed: yes / no

Thank-you: "Thank you for your time and for telling us what you really thought."
